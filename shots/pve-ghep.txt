  Buoc tiep:
   1. Ghi file ISO nay ra USB (tu 8 GB tro len) bang Rufus: chon file -> START -> hoi thi chon 'Write in DD Image mode'.
   2. Cam USB vao MAY CHU (da bat Virtualization trong BIOS, co cap mang), khoi dong tu USB. Sau 10 giay may TU CAI Proxmox (5-15 phut),
      tu khoi dong lai. DE NGUYEN USB den khi web bao may mau da co (may chu chep ISO may mau tu USB).
   3. Web CloudBlue > Quan tri > VPS ao hoa > May chu: bam DUYET may moi. May chu tu dung may mau (moi ban 20-40 phut).
   ISO nay XOA SACH o dia dau tien cua may khoi dong tu no (tru may dang co Windows / da co Proxmox: khong tu cai). Giu USB can than.
